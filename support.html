Privacy summary

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Codex: Cheats & Guide is an unofficial fan-made companion app. It is not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

What we collect

Nothing. Codex doesn't ask for an account, and it doesn't collect, store or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

Your checklist progress, completed missions, favorites and settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Content downloads

The app downloads its guide content from GitHub and content-delivery networks (GitHub Pages, jsDelivr). Like any website, these services may see your IP address when the file is requested. We don't receive or keep that information.

Purchases

Optional purchases (Codex Pro and tips) are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether a purchase is active.

Notifications

If you turn on alerts, notifications are created on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

Children

Codex doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/leonida-content/leonida-content.
